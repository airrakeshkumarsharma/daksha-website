How to request a cancellation or refund

Contact us by WhatsApp or call at +91 70758 59034, with your invoice number and the reason. We will confirm within 2 working days whether the request is approved.

How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days of approval.
  • Payments made online are refunded to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet) through the payment gateway. Depending on your bank, it can take a further 5–10 working days to appear.
  • Payments made by cash or bank transfer are refunded by bank transfer or UPI to an account in the payer's name.
  • Where GST was charged, a credit note is issued.

Failed or duplicate payments

If money left your account but the payment failed, or you were charged twice, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank or the gateway within 5–7 working days. If it isn't, contact us with the transaction reference and we will get it refunded.

Cancellation by us

If we cannot deliver what you ordered — for example an item is out of stock — we will tell you and refund any amount paid in full.
