FAQ

Good questions

Do you sell refurbished computers?

Ask us. If we offer a refurbished unit, it is labelled as refurbished on the quote and the invoice, with its own warranty terms written down.

Will you set up my billing printer and barcode scanner?

Yes. Printer, thermal printer and barcode scanner setup is part of the installation when you buy from us, and available as a service otherwise.

Do you deliver outside Samastipur?

We deliver and install in and around Samastipur. For other places, contact us first and we will tell you whether we can.
