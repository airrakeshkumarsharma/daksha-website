Yes. Tap "Add to list" on each item, then send the whole list to us on WhatsApp in one message.

Yes. Every sale gets a GST invoice (GSTIN 10GPXPD6272M1ZC).

Report it within 7 days of delivery. Items that are dead or defective on arrival are replaced or repaired; after that, the manufacturer's warranty applies and we help you claim it. See the Warranty & Returns policy.

Do you deliver?

We deliver and install in and around Samastipur. You can also collect from us.
