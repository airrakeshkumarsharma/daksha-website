FAQ

Questions people ask us

Where are you located?

We are in Samastipur, Bihar. See the contact page for the full address and directions.

Do you work only with shops?

No. We work with shops, offices, schools, coaching institutes and anyone who needs computers, repairs, software or a website.

Do you sell keyboards, mice and other accessories?

Yes. Keyboards, mice, cables, chargers, pen drives, hard disks, RAM, routers, printers, headphones, speakers and more. See the products page and tap Enquire to get the current price on WhatsApp.

Do you install CCTV cameras?

Yes. We visit the site, plan camera positions, supply the cameras and recorder, do the wiring and set up viewing on your phone, for shops, homes, offices and schools.

Does your billing software need internet?

No. It runs on your own computer and keeps working offline. An optional copy to your own Google Drive needs internet only when it syncs.

How do I pay?

By UPI, bank transfer, cash at the shop or online payment links. Every payment gets a GST invoice.

Do you give a GST invoice?

Yes. We are GST registered (GSTIN 10GPXPD6272M1ZC) and every sale or service is invoiced.
